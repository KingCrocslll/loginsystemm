‘Grateful to finally have him home’: Whānau of soldier killed in Ukraine speak out
Karanama Ruru
March 20, 2025 . 03:54
Farewell ceremony held in Kyiv for NZ soldier killed in Ukraine WarJohn Raoux / AP


The whānau of Corporal Dominic Abelen, killed in Ukraine in August 2022 have spoken out, after the 28 year old’s body was returned to NZ.

Bryce, Abelen’s father, said the family assumed the worst, that Abelen was buried in a mass grave.

“So to find out that he had been handed over and would be coming home was such a relief,” he said.

“This is the final part of this episode and we are so grateful to finally have him home.”

Bryce said in the time since Dominic’s death, the family has learned he was leading a company of eight soldiers when he died.

He was holding off the Russians while everyone else got out.

Abelen, 28, was killed in Ukraine while on leave without pay from the New Zealand Defence Force. He had not told the Defence Force he was travelling to Ukraine to fight.

Bryce, Abelen’s father, said the family assumed the worst, that Abelen was buried in a mass grave.

“What we do know is that his body had been in Kiev in Ukraine for a while, which we didn’t know about until we were contacted to do a DNA match. Because of the amount of people who need to be identified, this has taken a long time,” Bryce Abelen said.

“I was put in contact with the Weatherman Foundation who help to repatriate people and help those like me who might be in the same situation. It was a long process to have him identified and it took nearly six months to be in a position to be able to do a DNA test.“

Bryce said he had a call from Ukraine that told him there was an initial positive match. They had to do another witnessed DNA test for one hundred percent identification that this was Abelen.

“In an interview before his death, Dominic said he was in Ukraine for the fight between evil and good, and that he couldn’t just stand back at watch it. He was a determined man, he’s always been like that,” he said.
